FastCaloSim::LayerRoles struct
#include <LayerRoles.h>

The roles that calorimeter layers play in the parametrizations and that the cell geometry does not describe. They depend on the detector, so the host sets them with CaloGeo::set_layer_roles(); a parametrization that needs them throws if they were not set.

std::vector<std::size_t> strip_layers
Strip layers: the electromagnetic layers finely segmented in eta (ATLAS: EMB1 and EME1). TFCSEnergyAndHitGANV2 places the hits of hadrons 1 mm apart along the azimuth there instead of 5 mm, and does not limit them to 10 per voxel and direction.
std::vector<ShowerCenterLayer> shower_center_layers
The layers the binned showers take the shower center from, in order: the first one at which the particle's |eta| is not above its max_abs_eta, the last one (whose max_abs_eta is not used) if there is none (ATLAS: EMB2 up to |eta| = 1.4, else EME2)
std::vector<std::size_t> linear_upscaling_layers
Layers in which the upscaling of the binned showers samples the radius within a sub-bin from a linear distribution instead of a uniform one (ATLAS: EMB2)
std::vector<double> out_of_calo_report_threshold
Per layer, the expected energy (MeV) above which TFCSEnergyRenormalization reports that no cell of the layer received any of it (ATLAS: 8 times the approximate cell noise, 1500 MeV in EME1 to EME3). Layers beyond the end are not reported.
std::vector<std::vector<PhiModulationPeriod>> phi_modulation_periods
Per layer, the azimuthal periods of the energy response, in increasing max_abs_eta: the first one whose max_abs_eta is above the particle's |eta| applies, the last one if there is none (ATLAS: the accordion absorbers, 2 pi / 1024 in EMB, 2 pi / 768 in the outer and 2 pi / 256 in the inner wheel of the endcap, divided at |eta| = 2.5). TFCSPhiModulationCorrection and the binned showers use it for the position of a hit within the period.
