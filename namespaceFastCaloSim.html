auto FastCaloSim::gauss_ziggurat(CLHEP::HepRandomEngine* engine, double mean, double sigma) -> double
#include <FastCaloSim/Core/GaussZiggurat.h>

Gaussian random number from CLHEP's fast Ziggurat generator. The generator works in single precision: mean and sigma are converted to float and the result back to double, as the parametrizations have always done.

#include <FastCaloSim/Core/TFCSLateralShapeParametrizationHitBase.h>
template<typename T>
auto FastCaloSim::eta_jakobi(T eta) -> double

|dtheta/deta| = 1/cosh(eta). Together with dist000 it converts a distance in eta at the given eta into mm: delta_mm = delta_eta * eta_jakobi * dist000. Evaluated in the precision of the argument (float or double).

#include <FastCaloSim/Definitions/FloatCompare.h>
template<std::floating_point T>
auto FastCaloSim::exactly_equal(T a, T b) -> bool constexpr

Exact comparison of two floating-point numbers, for the cases where exact equality is meant: a value that is exactly zero (e.g. set to zero to mark a special case) or ties between values computed in the same way. -Wfloat-equal is suppressed only here, so that it still reports unintended exact comparisons everywhere else.

auto FastCaloSim::interpolate_phi(double phi1, double phi2, double w) -> double
#include <FastCaloSim/Definitions/Phi.h>

The azimuth (1 - w) * phi1 + w * phi2 along the shorter arc between phi1 and phi2, in [-pi, pi]: the plain interpolation unless the two angles lie on different sides of the +-pi seam
