FastCaloSim::LineStream class
#include <MLogging.h>

Contents

  • Reference

Collects the text of one FCS_MSG(lvl) << ... statement and adds it to the open line of this thread when the statement ends. A statement nested in another one (a function called in the expression that streams itself) therefore finishes first and does not mix with the outer text.
